## ✨ What it does

- **13 Research-Validated UX Scales**
- **Automatic Scoring** (faithful to original academic methods)
- **Privacy-First** — all data stays in your browser
- **Export Results** as **PDF, CSV, or TXT** (bundled as a ZIP)
- **Mobile-Responsive** and touch-friendly
- **Linkable scales** (share direct questionnaire URLs)

## 📊 Available Scales (shareable routes)

> Each scale has its own URL

### Usability Scales

* **System Usability Scale (SUS)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/sus](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/sus)
  10 items · 5-point Likert · Score 0–100

* **Usability Metric for User Experience (UMUX)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/umux](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/umux)
  4 items · 7-point Likert · Score 0–100

* **UMUX-Lite**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/umux-lite](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/umux-lite)
  2 items · 7-point Likert · Score 0–100

* **Computer System Usability Questionnaire (CSUQ)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/csuq](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/csuq)
  16 items · 7-point Likert · Subscales supported

* **Post-Study System Usability Questionnaire (PSSUQ)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/pssuq](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/pssuq)
  16 items · 7-point Likert · Lab-based assessment

---

### Task-Level Measures

* **Single Ease Question (SEQ)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/seq](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/seq)
  1 item · 7-point scale

* **After-Scenario Questionnaire (ASQ)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/asq](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/asq)
  3 items · 7-point Likert

---

### Comprehensive UX Measures

* **USE Questionnaire**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/use](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/use)
  30 items · 4 subscales · 7-point Likert

* **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/tam](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/tam)
  12 items · Perceived usefulness & ease of use · 7-point Likert

---

### Satisfaction & Loyalty

* **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/nps](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/nps)
  1 item · 0–10 scale · Score −100 to 100

* **Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/csat](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/csat)
  5 items · 5-point Likert · Score 0–100

---

### Generic Measures

* **Self-Reported Learnability**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/learnability](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/learnability)
  6 items · 7-point Likert

* **Basic UX Satisfaction Survey**
  🔗 [https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/basic-ux](https://cfunkz.github.io/UX-Scale-Lab/#/basic-ux)
  8 items · 5-point Likert
